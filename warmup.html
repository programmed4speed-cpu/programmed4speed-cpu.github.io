The 10-Minute Speed Warm-Up

A warm-up you can run with a whole team before practice or a game. It gets athletes moving, loosens the hips and ankles, and wakes up their first step, so they show up to the first rep ready to be fast.

  • About 10 minutes
  • No equipment
  • Any sport
  • 20–30 yards of space

1. Get warm · 3 min

01Easy jog or shuffleAround the field, court or a few lines of cones.2 min
02Leg swingsHold a fence or partner. Front to back, then side to side.10 each way

2. Move well · 4 min

03Walking lunge with a reachLong step, tall chest, reach both arms overhead.5 each leg
04A-skipsKnee up, toe up, punch the ground under the hips.2 × 15 yd
05High kneesQuick and light. Stay tall, arms driving.2 × 15 yd
06CariocaHips turn, shoulders stay square. Both directions.2 × 15 yd

3. Turn on speed · 3 min

07Pogo hopsStiff ankles, bounce off the balls of the feet, short ground time.2 × 10
08Falling startsLean forward until you have to step, then sprint. Push the ground back.3 × 10 yd
09Build-upsStart easy and build to about 80–90%. Walk back to recover.2 × 30 yd

Coaching cues: "Push the ground back, not down." "Tall chest, quick feet." "Arms drive the legs."

Tip: Keep it moving with little standing around. The goal is warm and sharp, not tired.
