The Acceleration Day Speed Warm-Up

This warm-up is built for an acceleration practice. It goes in order: get warm, open up the joints, stretch, run through movement drills, then turn on speed, and it finishes with full-effort accelerations so athletes are primed for starts and first-step work. Run it with the whole team before practice.

  • About 20 minutes
  • Acceleration practice
  • No equipment
  • Any sport
  • Track or field

1. Get warm · 3 min

01Jog around the trackOne easy lap. Relaxed, just get the blood moving.1 lap (400 m)

2. Joint mobility · 2 min

02Ankle circlesToe on the ground, roll the ankle both directions.10 each way
03Knee circlesFeet together, hands on knees, small circles.10 each way
04Hip circlesHands on hips, big slow circles.10 each way
05Arm circlesSmall to big, forward then backward.10 each way

3. Dynamic stretching · 4 min

06Leg swingsHold a fence or partner. Front to back, then side to side.10 each way
07Walking lunge with a reachLong step, tall chest, reach both arms overhead.5 each leg
08Grass pickersWalk, step forward, hinge at the hips with a flat back and scoop the ground. Feel the hamstring.2 × 15 yd
09Knee pullsWalk and pull one knee to the chest, rise up on the opposite toe.2 × 15 yd

4. Movement drills · 3 min

10Carioca (no step-over)Low, quick feet crossing in front and behind. Hips turn, shoulders stay square. Both directions.2 × 15 yd
11Carioca with step-overSame drill, but drive the trailing knee up and over on the front cross. Both directions.2 × 15 yd
12A-skipsKnee up, toe up, punch the ground under the hips.2 × 15 yd
13High kneesQuick and light. Stay tall, arms driving.2 × 15 yd

5. Turn on speed · 3 min

14Pogo hopsStiff ankles, bounce off the balls of the feet, short ground time.2 × 10
15Falling startsLean forward until you have to step, then sprint. Push the ground back.3 × 10 yd
16Build-upsStart easy and build to about 80–90%. Walk back to recover.2 × 30 yd

6. Accelerations · 4 min

17AccelerationsTwo-point stance, explode out at full effort. Stay low for the first few steps and push the ground back. Walk back for full recovery.4 × 20 yd

Coaching cues: "Push the ground back, not down." "Tall chest, quick feet." "Arms drive the legs."

Tip: Stretching and drills come before any speed work. Keep it moving through sections 1–4, then give full rest on the accelerations. The goal is warm and sharp, not tired.
