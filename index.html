  2. Send it inDM the clips to @programmed4speed or email them, with your sport and event.

How to film a clip I can break down

  • Full body, head to toeKeep your whole body in frame for the entire rep, feet included. Don't zoom in or crop.
  • Side view, from a distanceFilm from the side, square to your lane, about 10 to 15 yards away. For starts, add a second clip from behind.
  • Phone steady and sidewaysFilm horizontal at hip height. Use a tripod or prop the phone on something. If a friend films, they should pan smoothly with you.
  • Slow motion if you can120 or 240 fps is best, 60 fps works. No filters, speed edits, or music over the clip.
  • Good lightFilm in daylight or on a well-lit track, and don't shoot into the sun.
  • Fitted clothesWear fitted clothes in a color that stands out from the background, so your hips, knees, and ankles are easy to see.
  • Send the original videoUpload the file from your camera roll, not a screen recording or a story repost, so it stays sharp.
  • Tell me the contextYour sport, event, what the drill or rep is, your current PR, any injuries, and what you want me to look at.

Send 2 or 3 reps of the same thing when you can. Seeing the pattern repeat makes the breakdown more accurate.

Questions

FAQ

I play football, soccer, or basketball. Is this for me?

Yes. Acceleration, top speed, and jumping power carry into every sport, and your plan is written around your season.

Do I need a gym?

No. Every session has a version you can do on a track, a field, or at home.

Which program should I pick?

Meet in under a month: Jumpstart. Most athletes: the 5-Week Builder. Peaking for a big race: Full Speed. Want more explosiveness: add Plyo Power.

Can I combine programs?

Yes. Plyo Power runs alongside any speed program, and the plan is adjusted so your total training load stays right.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Move to a longer program and pay only the difference.

Do you train in person?

Limited in-person sessions are available on weekends in Tampa, FL. DM us to ask about times.
