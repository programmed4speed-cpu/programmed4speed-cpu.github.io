Process

How it works

  1. Pick a program and any add-ons.
  2. Pay and fill out the intake form.
  3. Receive your personalized plan within 48 hours.
  4. Do your starting speed test and begin week 1.
  5. Send check-ins and questions by email as you go.
  6. Retest at the end and see how much faster you got.

Before you start

Intake checklist

Have this information ready before you fill out the intake form.

  • Your age, grade, and main events
  • Current PRs and the meets where you ran them
  • How many years you have been sprinting
  • Days per week and time per session you can train
  • Access to a track, gym, or hills
  • Current or past injuries
  • Your target meet and its date

Questions

FAQ

Do I need a gym?

No. Every strength session has a bodyweight version you can do at home or on the track.

Can I follow this during my school season?

Yes. Put your team practice schedule on the intake form and the plan will be written around it so you are not overtraining.

Which program should I pick?

If your meet is less than a month away, pick the Jumpstart. The 5-Week Builder fits most athletes, and the 7-Week program is for peaking for a big race.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Upgrade to a longer program and pay only the difference.

Is this for beginners?

Yes. Plans match your experience level, from first-year athletes to experienced sprinters.

I play football or soccer. Is this for me?

Yes. Acceleration and top speed carry straight into any sport, and the plan is written around your season.

The fine print

Policies

  • Payment is due before your plan is written.
  • Plans are delivered within 48 hours of your completed intake form.
  • Refunds are available before your plan is delivered. After delivery, programs are non-refundable, but you can request changes to fit your schedule.
  • Athletes under 18 need a parent or guardian to approve the purchase and the plan.
  • Talk to a doctor before starting a new training program, especially with an injury or medical condition. Stop any exercise that causes pain.
